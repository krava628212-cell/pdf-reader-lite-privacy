Privacy Policy for PDF Reader Lite

Last updated: October 28, 2026

PDF Reader Lite is developed by SK Labs and works locally on the user's device.

Data processed

The app accesses only PDF files selected by the user. It stores local reading history, the last opened page, favorites, and annotations created by the user.

How data is used

PDF files and these settings are used only to read, edit, and restore the user's session. They are not sent to the developer or third parties.

Collection and sharing

PDF Reader Lite has no accounts, advertising, analytics, servers, internet features, or tracking. The app does not collect, sell, or share personal data.

Storage and deletion

Data is stored in the app's private storage on the device. The user can clear the reading history in the app or uninstall the app to remove local data.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the developer contact details shown on the PDF Reader Lite Google Play listing.
